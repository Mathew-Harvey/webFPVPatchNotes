# Release notes, 31 October to 3 November 2026

Written from what reached `main` of the simulator (pull requests 208 to 217, 219 and 220), the board (pull requests 63, 64, 66 and 67) and the front page (commit f415510), read on 3 November 2026. Times are Perth time (AWST). Chapter 7's notes ended on 30 October. Chapter 6 (Twenty two new maps, 31 October) already tells the maps of 31 October, so this file starts after them with what changed about the maps on 1 November. The simulator is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator`, the board is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator-LeaderBoard` and the front page is `Mathew-Harvey/landingpage-WebFPVSimulator-`.

The book's version of these notes is chapter 8, Two new worlds, in `docs/chapters.js`. Every bullet ends with where it came from.

## New maps

- **Hanabi Sound (3 November).** A dance festival at dusk: a main stage with a lit screen and a roof of lights, a crowd at the barrier, lasers and fireworks, a sound desk in its tent, a ferris wheel, bars, food trucks and named gaps to thread, including one under the decks and one through the big wheel. Simulator PR 219, board PR 66.
- **Yumeboshi Land (3 November).** A theme park with a roller coaster to fly: a chain lift, a drop, a loop, a helix and a tunnel through the dragon mountain, with a train on the track. A gate, a main street of shops, a hub, a castle, a lake with swan boats. The big wheel, carousel, drop tower, teacups, swing ride and swinging ship move and are solid where they are drawn. Simulator PR 220, board PR 67.

## Rooms and mini games

- **Quad Ball rooms (1 November).** The match clock runs in a room, each pilot starts at their own team's end, and every mini game asks how you want to play: with bots, make a game or join with a code. Simulator PR 208.
- **Rematch (1 November).** When a match ends everybody gets Rematch, Fly on or Leave, and more than half the pilots asking starts the next match. A pilot who leaves keeps a bot in their seat, and a pilot left alone gets a bot to play. Board PR 63, simulator PR 208.
- **The title cover (1 November).** It asks Fly solo or Multiplayer, and Mini games moved to a panel on the Fly solo page. Simulator PR 211.

## Sound

- **Softer sounds (2 November).** Every synthesised cue is rounder: sine cues, a gate click and a crash with slower starts and less top, gentler menu taps and a steadier, lower wind. Simulator PR 216.
- **A deep thock (2 November).** Landing and take off are a deep thock, and the motors have more body. Simulator PR 217.

## Phone play

- **Thumb stick size (1 November).** A Settings row on touchscreens: Small, Normal or Large sticks. Simulator PR 209.
- **Fire buttons (2 November).** In the mini games the GUN and MISSILE buttons follow the stick size, and they fade away on the ground and come back in the air. Simulator PR 215.

## The manga look

- **Drawn lines, conifers and grass (1 November).** On the new maps, cedars, firs and pines are drawn as layers of points, the grass is inked in tufts, and far pencil ink carries the lines across a valley. Simulator PR 210.
- **Fly notes (1 November).** Shiokaze Point's sea has a steady swell and its promenade rail is posts and bars. Hoshino Shoji, Raicho Ridge, Hotaru Valley, Kumoi Towers, Tsukimi Danchi, Shiroyama FMX and Kamome Wharf are solid where they are drawn, so a gap you can see is a gap you can fly. Simulator PR 212.
- **Inked sea and rock seams (1 November).** Hyoura Island's sea is inked swell in cold tones, and Shiokaze Point's cliffs show rock seams. Simulator PR 213.
- **Momiji Pass and Hayate Circuit (1 November).** Momiji Pass's car park is worn asphalt with repair patches. Hayate Circuit's pit roof has seams, a walkway with a yellow safety line and its name painted on. Simulator PR 214.

## The board and the front page

- **The Maps page (1 November).** The board's Maps page takes its worlds from the simulator's own list, so no world is missing from it. Board PR 64.
- **Konex Drone (1 November).** On the front page as our Spanish partner, in the partner row. Front page commit f415510.
