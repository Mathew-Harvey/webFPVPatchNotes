# Release notes, 3 November to 6 November 2026

Written from what reached `main` of the simulator (pull requests 221 to 238), the board (pull requests 68 to 78) and the front page (commit 076836f), read on 6 November 2026. Times are Perth time (AWST). Chapter 8's notes ended on 3 November with Hanabi Sound and Yumeboshi Land. The simulator is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator`, the board is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator-LeaderBoard` and the front page is `Mathew-Harvey/landingpage-WebFPVSimulator-`.

The book's version of these notes is chapter 9, Track of the week, in `docs/chapters.js`. Every bullet ends with where it came from.

## Two places rebuilt

- **Thomas More's Shop, laid out again (4 November).** Read again from the real building's photos and flight frames: the dock doors in the right wall, the racks on the right sides, the office at the aisle's end, the machine room bigger. Simulator PR 228, board PR 71.
- **The paint shop and the workshop (4 November).** The paint shop has its one door, the workshop floor has its tables and cabinet towers, and the racking is full. Simulator PR 229, board PR 72.
- **The print room and the booths (4 November).** Behind the grey door is the print room, an office and a little white room, with no ceilings, so you can climb over the walls and look in. Two plain booths stand on the floor to fly through, and new lines go under the racking, through the forklift's cab and down a cardboard tube. Simulator PR 230.
- **The floor plan (4 November).** A white block of rooms walled to the roof, a red corridor behind the south racking to the washroom, the exit door by the barani corner, and a sweep that makes sure no wall can be flown through. Simulator PR 232, board PR 74.
- **An FPV VIP's house, from two flights (4 November).** Rebuilt from two real flights through it: the ground floor, the basement, a back yard with a tall fence and a pavilion in the corner of the house and the garage. Simulator PR 233, board PR 75.
- **The inside (4 November).** The family room's wood built ins and a grey sectional, and a guest room downstairs with a second door. Simulator PR 234.
- **The outside (5 November).** Two maples, the houses next door and over the back fence, and the upstairs windows where the flights show them. Simulator PR 235, board PR 76.

## Five inch racing

- **Gates and flags that read across the field (3 November).** Gates are printed race orange with their chequers, and the flags are yellow and orange. Simulator PR 221.
- **Six new tracks (3 November).** In the builder's list, each with slaloms, spirals, split S, loops and dives. Simulator PR 221.
- **A pilots' corner (3 November).** A shade, the timing table and a net behind a five inch track's start pads, all solid. Simulator PR 221.
- **Race pack and setup sheet (3 November).** A five inch track's sheet on the board has a race pack with the plan and the kit list, and a setup sheet to print. Board PR 68.
- **Night races (3 November).** In the builder a five inch track can be set to Night: a moon and stars, every gate outlined in LED strip and the flag masts lit. Simulator PR 222.
- **Race time (3 November).** A Settings row that flies any five inch track by day or at night, or at the time its builder picked. Simulator PR 226.
- **The pilots' corner net (3 November).** It no longer streaks, and its canopy is solid where it is drawn. Simulator PR 223.

## Track of the week and race helpers

- **Track of the week (5 November).** Fly solo has a card for one official track each week, read from the board. The best lap by Monday wins the week. Simulator commit e241a8a (PR 238), board PR 78.
- **Board top 3 (5 November).** A Ghost choice that flies the three fastest laps on the board with you at once. Simulator commit e241a8a.
- **Your gap at every gate (5 November).** With the ghost off, each gate reads your gap to your own best lap. Simulator commit e241a8a.
- **Reset to: Last gate (5 November).** R and auto reset put you down just past the last gate you flew through. Simulator commit e241a8a.
- **Prop knocks (5 November).** A knock on a prop alone arms auto reset, and a pilot climbing out upright is let go. Simulator commit e241a8a.

## The camera

- **Fisheye (5 November).** A row under Field of view, Off, Slight, Medium or Strong, off until you pick it. Only your own camera bends, and the mini game markers bend with it. Simulator PR 236.
- **The career's marker (5 November).** The career's target marker bends with the fisheye too. Simulator commit aae8534 (PR 238).

## Rooms

- **Your own made up handle (4 November).** On the pilot editor's Tag tab, pick a first word, a second word and a number. Listed, school safe and streamer rooms still never show a typed name. Simulator PR 231, board PR 73.

## Supporters

- **369DRONE (5 November).** A Japanese FPV brand supporting WebFPV. Its sign is in the freestyle maps to find. Simulator PR 237, board PR 77. On the front page it is in the footer's Supporters block, beside YourFPV. Front page commit 076836f.

## Maps and radios

- **Hanabi Sound, more cartoon (3 November).** A rounder crowd, round trees and pods on the big wheel. Simulator PR 224, board PR 69.
- **Yumeboshi Land, more cartoon (3 November).** Its own trees, striped awnings and a round topped castle arch. Simulator PR 225, board PR 70.
