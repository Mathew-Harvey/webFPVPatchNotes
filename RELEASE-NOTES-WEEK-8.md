# Release notes, 28 to 30 October 2026

Written from what reached `main` of the simulator (pull requests 171, 173 and 177 to 185, and the owner's direct pushes, linked as commits), read on 1 November 2026. Times are Perth time (AWST). Chapter 5's notes ended on 28 October with the quad picker and the career skill tree still open; both are on `main` now and are in this chapter. Week 7's notes were never written into this repository, so this file follows week 6's.

The book's version of these notes is chapter 7, Game night, in `docs/chapters.js`. Every bullet ends with where it came from: the simulator is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator`.

## Mini games

- **Dogfight (29 October).** Guns, missiles and bots on any map, alone or in a room. Up to seven bots from rookie to ace, each with its own habits, and missiles that can be dodged. The host fills a room's roster with bots. Simulator PR 179, rooms commit 28f2165.
- **Dogfight on a phone (29 October).** GUN and MISSILE sit beside the right thumb plate. Simulator commit cb4bacc.
- **Ribbon cutting (29 October).** Every quad trails a ribbon. Cut the others, keep yours, and the last pilot with a ribbon wins. Alone with bots and in rooms with the host's bots. Simulator PR 180, which reached `main` with PR 179; the mini games bug hunt is PR 181.
- **Goat herding with bots (29 October).** Most goats home wins the round. In a room there is one herd for everybody and the host's bots join in. Simulator commits 3e2c46c, 0231461 and 0967468, and 7edea00 for hosting.
- **Drift Matsuri with bots (29 October).** Everyone films the cars and the best reel wins, alone and in rooms. Simulator commits e9d5b0c and 0967468.
- **Quad Ball (30 October).** Rocket League with quads. Pink against mint, from one against one to three against three, with bots or in a room. A stadium with stands, a crowd and a cage of light, goal fireworks and a trail behind a fast ball. Simulator PR 184.
- **Ridgeline MX (30 October).** A motocross chase cam on the Mini games page: ten bikes in three packs, you film them for a minute and the best reel wins. A finish arch, bunting, marshal huts and advertising boards along the start straight. Simulator PR 185, commits 9477fa8 and 90de503.

## Career and quads

- **The career skill tree (28 October).** A trunk to the Rookie Licence, then Race or Freestyle. 73 lessons in 18 parts, many flown in the real maps, 22 new lessons, a livery for every part you finish and a career title beside your name in a room. Simulator PR 171.
- **The quad picker (28 October).** Fly the five inch or the whoop on any freestyle world; the page says which the map was built for. Simulator PR 173 and commit 0f57f60.
- **Everything follows the quad you pick (28 October).** Props in your view, your chase view and the other pilots in a room are drawn at the size of the quad you chose. Simulator PR 178.

## Maps and small things

- **Patron goats (29 October).** One extra goat on the goat herding map for each patron. Simulator PR 182.
- **Konex Drone (29 October).** A small Konex Drone logo is hidden in every map. Simulator PR 183.
- **Sunnyfield set dressing (28 October).** Window boxes, patio sets, grills, bins, bikes and more, on every house and shop. Simulator PR 177.
- **The take card (30 October).** Tap it to dismiss it, and its labels follow the map. Simulator commit 6722107.
- **A craft wedged at a wall (29 October).** One resting at the foot of a wall is set down. Simulator commit d431970.
