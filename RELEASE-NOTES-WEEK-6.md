# Release notes, 26 to 28 October 2026

Written from what reached `main` of the simulator (pull requests 153 to 176 and the owner's direct pushes, linked as commits), read on 28 October 2026. Times are Perth time (AWST). Open pull requests are left out: the quad picker (173) and the career skill tree (171). Most of it has been checked by scripts and a headless browser only.

The book's version of these notes is chapter 5, Rooms and mini games, in `docs/chapters.js`. Every bullet ends with where it came from: the simulator is `Mathew-Harvey/WebFPVSimulator`.

## Fly together

- **Rooms are open to everybody (27 October).** Fly together on the title, a Rooms tab, a code or a link. Nobody is matched with strangers and a solo flight is never entered. Quick lines and waves; typed chat only in a private room whose host turns it on. Racing with a pit, Ready, a GO countdown, standings and results. Simulator PR 153 and commit bde7163.
- **The pilot builder (27 October).** A trading card with tabs Face, Hair, Eyes, Gear and Quad, try on by hover, Random, Undo and Reset. Simulator PR 156. More hair, glasses and tattoos on a Marks tab, PR 157.
- **Link pictures and watching (27 October).** A room link shows the host's pilot on the map, and V watches another pilot from behind their quad. Simulator PR 158, sweep PR 159.
- **The host moves the room by vote (27 October).** Simulator PR 162.
- **The Follow question (28 October).** A paper panel across the middle of the screen with Follow and Stay. Simulator PR 165.
- **Overview camera (28 October).** O opens a camera over the whole field with trails, R, F, H and a keys sheet. Simulator PR 175.

## Maps and mini games

- **90%er goat herding (28 October).** A whoop map: Heads' house and a neighbourhood, a goat for every sticker, herd them into the garage, cars on the streets. Simulator PR 167.
- **Drift Matsuri (28 October).** A festival drift circuit where you film the battles, scored and graded S to D. Simulator PR 170, fence fix PR 176.
- **Sunnyfield (28 October).** A cartoon town with a clock tower, a water tower, a dump and twelve named gaps. Simulator PR 172.
- **Thomas More's Shop (27 October).** A whoop map built from his own flights, now official. Simulator PR 161 and commit 56f7c4d.

## Settings and fixes

- **Props and the quad's shadow in the FPV view (28 October).** A Props and shadow row turns either off. Simulator PR 164.
- **Field of view (27 October).** Beside Camera angle in Settings and Rates. Simulator PR 163.
- **Keyboard sticks (28 October).** Ramp steadily from cruise to full while a key is held. Simulator PR 174.
